HOW TO PLAY: Move with the cross pad, arrow keys, or tap the field. SWORD strikes — every hero's attacks carry his own names. RELEASE looses SEVEN POWER ORBS that hunt the enemy (about 4 HP each). Solve KJV scrolls for keys, take communion to heal, walk saved souls into Acts Mission Church, then go through the GATE. SAVE gives you a short code (like AM-780). Open MOVES to buy new special and melee moves with coins and choose which ones appear on your screen — up to 3 SPECIAL moves and 4 MELEE moves at a time.
